Welcome to Brikama Methodist Academy

We are dedicated to providing quality education and nurturing a love of learning in every student. Our experienced teachers and modern facilities create an environment where students thrive academically and personally.

Our mission

Under God, we seek to provide quality and affordable education, especially for children in Brikama and the surrounding areas.

Our vision

Through wholesome discipline, we produce citizens who are responsible to themselves, their community and their nation.
